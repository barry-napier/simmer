    How it works

    macOS has one volume for everything. Simmer adds one for each app.

    1. Click the sliders in your menu bar.Every app that is playing sound shows up, with its icon.
    2. Slide or mute.Each app gets louder or softer on its own. Your Mac’s main volume stays where it is.
    3. Forget about it.Simmer remembers each app’s volume. Next time that app makes a sound, it is already right.

    It hears your apps. It keeps nothing.

    Nothing recorded
    Simmer takes each app’s sound and plays it back at your volume, a few thousandths of a second later. It is never saved.
    Nothing sent
    No account, no server, no tracking. The only network use is checking for updates.
    Open code
    Every line is on GitHub, so you, or someone you trust, can check.

    Turn it down.

    1. Download Simmer and drag it to Applications.
    2. Open it. Click the sliders icon in the menu bar.
    3. When macOS asks, let Simmer hear other apps. That is how it changes their volume.
